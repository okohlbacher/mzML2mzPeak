  <action>
    In `src/sdrf/parse.rs` implement `parse_sdrf(path: &Path) -> Result<SdrfTable, SdrfError>` using

    as `Vec<String>` and each record as an `SdrfRow`. SDRF cells are UTF-8 already; do not Latin-1
