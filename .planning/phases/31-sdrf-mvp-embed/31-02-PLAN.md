`convert_mzml` (src/write/mzml.rs) — the plain-mzML path that today finalizes via a single

and the helper EXIST and keeps the no-SDRF output byte-identical. Plan 03 wires the flag + back-ref + test.

seam in the plain-mzML path. Getting the refactor green (with byte-identical no-SDRF output) de-risks the
flag + back-ref wiring in Plan 03.
