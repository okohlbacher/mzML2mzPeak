<objective>
Extend the single CV source of truth `src/schema/cv.rs` with the sample-metadata structural terms
(SMCVG-02, SMSPEC-02), and deliver the **Phase-31 carve-out**: the bare `entity_type: sample-metadata` /

(channel **role** attribute; **reporter-ion m/z** attribute) and confirms `MS:1002602` "sample label" +
its reagent children (TMT126…) are the channel-label terms — there is NO `channel_list` (RATIFIED-E).

constants Phase 31 can use without re-deciding.

Output: extended `src/schema/cv.rs` (new single-source accessors for the structural terms + the
entity_type/data_kind tokens, each with a no-drift test), and new `docs/cv-requests.md` rows for any
structural term that lacks a canonical PSI-MS accession.

This plan is GOVERNANCE/FOUNDATION ONLY — it declares stable tokens. No facet emits; the emitter that
uses these tokens lands in Phase 31 (SM-02/SM-04) and Phase 34 (CHAN-*).
