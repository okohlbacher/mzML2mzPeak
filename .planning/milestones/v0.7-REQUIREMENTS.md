# Requirements Archive: mzML2mzPeak — Milestone v0.7

**Milestone:** v0.7 — Upstream rebase, CV governance & spec-governed conformance hardening
**Shipped:** 2026-06-09 · **Tag:** `v0.7`
**Defined:** 2026-06-08 · **Closed:** 2026-06-09 (all 9 active requirements DONE)

**Core Value:** Convert an arbitrary imzML imaging dataset into a valid imaging mzPeak file without losing
spatial or spectral information — every pixel's coordinates and its m/z + intensity survive the roundtrip.

> v0.7 hardened the spec-governed round trip on a current-upstream base: the Phase-23 rebase + CV
> governance + declared-geometry threading + reverse provenance + L2 conformance. The SDRF
> sample-metadata + isobaric-channel cluster (Phase 27) and the upstreaming/de-vendoring work (Phases 22 +
> 29) were relocated to v0.8; the imaging-structure cluster (F6/F7/F8) was deferred beyond v1.0. v0.7
> carried NO new dependency.
