## Goal

Harden the spec-governed round trip on top of a current-upstream base. Adopt the upstream rebase first
(Phase 23 — bump the vendored `mzpeak_prototyping` + `mzdata` to current HEAD, dropping patches proven
fixed upstream), then establish single-source CV governance (Phase 24 — resolve the v0.6 `TODO(F9)` IMS
placeholders, drive the reverse `<cvList>` from `cv_list()` no-drift-by-construction, decode-by-CURIE),
thread declared imzML `<scanSettings>` geometry forward (Phase 25), copy `source_files[]` provenance back
into the reverse `.imzML` (Phase 26), and wire an L2 value-equal-under-recorded-transform conformance arm
into the CLI (Phase 28). Every change fed back into `docs/mzpeak-imaging-spec-suggestions.md` +
`schema/*.json` (the standing three-places rule) and queued for an end-of-milestone BATCH proposal to
`HUPO-PSI/mzPeak-specification` (narrowed to the v0.7-only items: cv_list + scan_settings_list/IMS
geometry + L2 transform-record). **v0.7 added NO new dependency.**
